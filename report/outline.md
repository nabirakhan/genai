# Report outline (IEEE two-column, ~6–8 pages)

Text is written in week 8. Every number comes from a tracked file; figures/tables are regenerated by
the scripts in `report/figures.md`.

1. **Abstract** – question, setup (APTOS, cond. StyleGAN2-ADA, 6 conditions x 4 fractions x 3 ratios, IDRiD), headline answer.
2. **Introduction** – rare severe DR; why GAN augmentation claims are weak (weak baselines, in-distribution tests, no data-size / ratio axis); contributions.
3. **Related work** – StyleGAN2 / ADA [1,2]; GAN augmentation for fundus images; FID limits for retinal images [3]; RandAugment [9], mixup [10]; fairness / pigmentation [4,5].
4. **Method**
   4.1 Data: APTOS 2019, crop/resize, duplicate removal (vessel correlation; why pHash failed, n dropped, grade conflicts), stratified 70/15/15, nested rare-grade fractions (table of counts), IDRiD external test (516).
   4.2 Generator: class-conditional StyleGAN2-ADA, transfer from FFHQ-256, shape-tolerant resume (fc0 / cmap), training budget per GAN, ADA target. Sampling: conditional truncation [Dobler et al., IJCAI 2022; StyleGAN-XL; StyleGAN-T], w' = w̄_c + ψ(w − w̄_c) with w̄_c from 10,000 latents per grade and ψ = 0.8, instead of the single running w_avg (52–58% grade 0, which would shift rare-grade samples ≈19–20% towards the other grades in W). Fixed before any classifier run. Figure idea: G.synthesis at ψ = 0 for the global w_avg vs w̄_3, w̄_4. Limitation: no study compares global vs class centres on downstream classification; ψ = 0.8 vs 1.0 untested (CAS, Ravuri & Vinyals 2019, shows the fidelity/diversity trade-off).
   4.3a Augmentation detail: condition 5 RandAugment is torchvision magnitude 9 of 31 bins, ≈ M = 3 on the RandAugment paper's 0–10 scale (shear ±0.09, translate ±13.6%, colour factors 1 ± 0.27 at 256 px).
   4.3 Classifier and conditions: EfficientNet-B0 at 256 px, optimiser, early stopping on val QWK, the six conditions, synthetic ratio definition, 3 seeds.
   4.4 Evaluation: macro F1, QWK, recall 3/4; bootstrap CIs; paired comparison (6) vs (5); IDRiD without retraining.
   4.5 Responsible-AI checks: metadata + visible label, NN memorisation, RPS.
5. **Experiments and results**
   5.1 GAN quality: FID/KID overall and per class; samples (Fig. samples); ADA vs no ADA at 10%.
   5.2 Main table at 100% (Table main_aptos_test / main_idrid).
   5.3 Learning curves over fractions (Fig. learning_curve_*).
   5.4 Synthetic ratio map (Fig. ratio_map_*).
   5.5 External test: APTOS vs IDRiD gaps.
   5.6 Memorisation and pigmentation (Fig. memorization_*, rps_hist).
   5.7 Grad-CAM qualitative (Fig. gradcam_examples).
6. **Discussion** – when the GAN helps (if ever), cost/benefit vs RandAugment + mixup, variance across seeds, small test sets.
7. **Limitations** – 256 px, short GAN budget, one architecture, India-only data (clinic/camera shift, not population), FID on small sets, RPS at 256 px.
